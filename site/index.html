Why this exists

Becoming Muslim is celebrated. What comes after is too often missed.

A national study of converts in the UK found that support for new Muslims in mosques is “poor at best and non-existent at worst”.

The brothers and sisters we have sat with say the same. Welcomed warmly on the day, then left to work it out alone.

~100kBritons estimated to have come to Islam by 2010
~5,200estimated new Muslims every year
2 in 3of those converts were women
< 28average age of a convert

Source: Faith Matters report by Swansea University researchers, January 2011. Estimates built from census data; Faith Matters itself called the headline figure an “intellectual guesstimate”.

What is Islam?

A short, honest overview. We follow the structure the Prophet ﷺ gave when Jibril asked him about the religion — Sahih Muslim 8.

Islamالإسلام
What we do

The five pillars: the testimony of faith, the five daily prayers, giving, fasting in Ramadan, and pilgrimage for those able.

Imanالإيمان
What we believe

Belief in Allah, His angels, His books, His messengers, the Last Day, and the divine decree.

Ihsanالإحسان
Doing it well

To worship Allah as though you see Him, and if you do not see Him, knowing that He sees you.

You do not have to take it all at once.

When the Prophet ﷺ sent Mu’adh to Yemen, he told him to call people first to the shahadah, then to prayer, then to zakat — step by step.

“Make things easy and do not make them hard.”

Why do some of us say “revert”?

Islam teaches every person is born on the fitrah, a natural state of turning to Allah (Quran 30:30). On that view, someone who comes to Islam is returning to it. “Convert” and “new Muslim” are fine too — use whichever word you are comfortable with.

Community

Someone to learn with. Somewhere to be.

A Revert Circle is a small, regular gathering of new and returning Muslims, hosted by a masjid. It is not a new organisation — it belongs to the masjid that runs it. Our first is live at Al Furqan, Manchester.
